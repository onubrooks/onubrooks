## What I work on

### Financial data engineering

I design production pipelines and controls for reconciliation, revenue and expense assurance, tax invoicing, and finance analytics. My focus is correctness at publication boundaries: deterministic processing, idempotent reruns, bounded backfills, explicit data-quality gates, and auditable outcomes.

### Data quality and reliability

I have built reusable, configuration-driven validation components that place checks at meaningful stages of a pipeline. These distinguish blocking failures from operational warnings and make failures actionable through structured diagnostics and alerting.

### AI-powered analytics

I build governed data assistants that combine metadata, approved business context, retrieval, and constrained SQL execution. I care about answer quality, context engineering, observability, and showing users the SQL and evidence behind an answer.

## How I approach engineering

1. Define the business grain and correctness conditions before choosing tools.
2. Make retries and backfills safe by design.
3. Put validation before irreversible publication.
4. Preserve enough lineage and evidence to explain every important number.
5. Use AI to accelerate engineering while keeping human judgment responsible for business logic and final verification.
